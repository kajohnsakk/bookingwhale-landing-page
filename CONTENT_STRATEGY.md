# BookingWhale content direction

Reviewed 24 October 2026 against https://bookingwhale.co/ and the landing page’s existing product illustrations.

## Intended reader and decision

A business owner evaluating a booking platform should be able to identify the item they sell or reserve, understand the customer and staff workflow, and know what to bring to a product demonstration.

## Changes

- Hero: explicit LINE OA booking positioning and bookable units (services, classes, scheduled access); clearly separates the customer entry point from the merchant management system.
- Product story: each sentence describes the action shown, progressing from customer date/time selection to merchant booking records and customer history.
- Industry gallery: replaces lists of business categories with concrete usage examples and schedule/capacity concepts. Restaurant remains one example. Tours replace accommodation in the example label because the visual shows a scheduled tour, not hotel inventory or a property management system.
- Setup: specifies existing LINE OA, what is bookable, available times, and capacity before sharing a booking link.
- Resources: frames the linked articles as practical examples of booking rounds and classes.
- FAQ: leads with fit, explains LINE OA versus merchant system, identifies available booking data, and gives a concrete demonstration preparation checklist.
- CTA: asks owners to bring their bookable items, times, and capacity; the email action requests a demonstration rather than implying an instant self-service demo opens.
- Metadata/footer: use specific booking-platform language rather than generic ease-of-use claims.

## Evidence boundaries

The live reference supports general LINE OA booking positioning and sector breadth. Its beta, pricing, availability, and support claims conflict, so no trial terms, numerical adoption figures, service levels, payment guarantees, or security certifications were added. Custom integrations, staffing/resource dependencies, availability, and pricing are explicitly subject to confirmation. Existing product illustrations and captures are unchanged, including illustrative table selection, tickets, and resource booking; these should not be treated as independent evidence that every specialist workflow ships unchanged for every customer.

## Review criteria

Owners can answer: what can customers book, where do they book, where do staff see it, which example resembles my business, and what happens after I contact the team. Marketing font sizes, layout, motion, section order, and application UI data are unchanged.
